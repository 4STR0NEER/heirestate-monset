Privacy notice

What happens to the details you send us with an enquiry.

Who we are

Heir Estates presents residential projects in Cebu. Enquiries are answered by Marilyn & Percival Laranjo, licensed real estate salespersons with Leuterio Realty & Brokerage (PRC licence 0026225, DHSUD registration R7-A-04/21-630). Write to heirestates.fh@gmail.com about anything in this notice.

What we receive

Only what you choose to send. The enquiry form on our sites does not send anything by itself: it opens your own email app with your enquiry written out, and nothing reaches us until you press send there. That email carries:

  • your name, mobile number and email address;
  • the project and unit type you are asking about, and a preferred viewing date if you gave one;
  • anything you wrote in your message.

Our sites do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

What we use it for

To answer your enquiry: to send the price list or floor plans you asked for, arrange a viewing, and follow up on that enquiry. We rely on the consent you give when you tick the box on the form, and you can withdraw it at any time.

Who sees it

The agents named above. Where you go ahead with a unit, the details needed for your reservation are shared with that project's developer. We do not sell your details or pass them to anyone else for marketing.

How long we keep it

For as long as your enquiry and any purchase that follows from it are open, and no longer than the law requires after that.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act 10173) you may ask to see the details we hold about you, have them corrected or deleted, or object to our using them. Write to the address above. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).
