## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Vite + React + Tailwind v4, Motion, Skiper/Vengeance components; Three.js and anime.js join for the loader.

## Users

International buyers and investors judging a developer's portfolio; the client and critics reviewing it are demanding.

## Product Purpose

Heir Estates showcases five property projects. Headline "Building your future, one property at a time"; subheader "Securing wealth, creating your legacy".

## Brand Commitments

- Palette: #30373E, #7A2038, #C49B4C, #0A4F54, #E2D2B4, plus Heir Navy #14213A (chosen with the user).
- Temporary logo: the Lit Window (H of two towers, the crossbar a lit gold window; right tower doubles as E).
- Hero: the five projects as windows side by side, all visible at once; unannounced projects are shuttered.
- English only for now; aesthetics first.

## Evidence on Hand

Live projects: Alto Ranudo, The Wave (Towers), Pristina Town. Source renders and walkthroughs in `resources/` (gitignored); web cuts in `public/media/`. No prices or dates confirmed yet; do not invent them. The Wave's agents (Marilyn & Percival Laranjo, Leuterio Realty) are the one confirmed contact, from their card in `resources/`, and appear on The Wave's site with the user's approval. The Wave has studio, one-, two- and three-bedroom dress-up photos and a one-bedroom walkthrough film.

Each project with its own site (`site: true` in `src/projects.js`) uses its building's colour as the accent on the house palette; The Wave's is its brochure blue, #31456A.
